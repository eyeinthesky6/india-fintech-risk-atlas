## Summary
UPI involves NPCI as scheme operator, participating banks, PSP banks, third-party applications and merchants. The responsibilities and available data differ by participant.

## Evidence from primary material
A bank may hold the account and execute payment operations while an application provides the user interface; do not treat all actors as interchangeable. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Participant-specific fraud monitoring, complaints, merchant onboarding and reconciliation require different control ownership.

## Limitations and false positives
The exact obligations of each actor must be traced to its scheme and regulatory documents.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
