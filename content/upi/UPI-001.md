# Unusual outbound payment velocity

**Status: draft — not a validated mandatory control.**

## Summary
An unusual concentration of outgoing payments may be a useful signal when considered alongside account history, device context, counterparty relationships and other evidence. Transaction volume alone does not prove fraud.

## How it can appear
An account makes a sudden series of transfers that differs from its normal activity. The context may be benign or may warrant investigation.

## Candidate checks
Compare recent activity with a customer's normal behavior, where permitted and technically feasible. Consider beneficiary diversity and contextual changes. Do not treat any unsourced numeric threshold as a universal requirement.

## Data and constraints
Payment event timestamps, transaction outcomes, pseudonymous customer and counterparty identifiers, account tenure and authorized device/context signals. Minimize collection and observe applicable data access rules.

## Legitimate lookalikes
Small merchants, group collections, family transfers, festive events, and new high-activity users.

## Response considerations
Prefer proportionate investigation and customer-protective measures; avoid automatic accusations or indiscriminate blocking based on one signal.
