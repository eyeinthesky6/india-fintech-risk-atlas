## Summary
Customers and merchants have complaint channels tied to their participating institutions.

## Evidence from primary material
NPCI FAQs describe customers raising grievances via UPI apps of participating banks and merchants contacting acquiring banks. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Record transaction references, status evidence, complaint acknowledgment and the responsible institution. Verify current dispute-specific timelines separately.

## Limitations and false positives
A pending status, failed transaction and unauthorized payment are different incident classes and should not share one generic resolution path.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
