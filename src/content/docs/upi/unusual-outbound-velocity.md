**ID:** UPI-001 · **Type:** Detective signal · **Basis:** Illustrative practice · **Status:** Research preview

## Pattern
A sudden concentration of outgoing payments can merit investigation when it differs from an account's normal activity. Velocity alone is not evidence of fraud.

## Candidate checks
Compare activity with relevant account history, transaction outcomes and counterparty diversity where data access permits. No universal threshold is recommended here.

## False positives
Small merchants, group collections, family transfers, festivals and newly active customers can produce unusual bursts.

## Applicability
Which bank or payment-system participant can implement a check depends on its role and available data. Relevant RBI security directions must be read for the correct entity class, effective date and section before describing a binding obligation.[1]
