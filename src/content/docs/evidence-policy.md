India Fintech Risk Atlas is a **reference platform first**; candidate solutions come second. Every record separates verified reference facts from company practices, practitioner accounts, academic paper leads, and editorial guidance across **five evidence layers**:

## Five evidence layers

| Layer | What it means | Typical attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Regulatory** | Regulator or scheme operator's requirement or guidance, scoped to applicable entities and dates | RBI/NPCI, document, section and date |
| **Industry practice** | Published company implementation or case study | Company and original article; `company-reported` or `vendor-reported` |
| **Community insight** | A named individual's firsthand public observation or operational lesson | Author, role, publication date and original link |
| **Research & academic** | Published empirical or academic studies (`arXiv`, workshops, benchmarks) exploring open gaps | Authors, paper ID/venue, dataset period, sample limits, and evaluation caveats |
| **Atlas guidance** | Editorial synthesis, operational gap framing, candidate controls, telemetry prerequisites, and validation guidance | Atlas synthesis, source links, caveats, and independent reviewer state |

Academic and empirical papers sit in their own **Research & academic** layer—separate from **Industry practice**, **Community insight**, and **Atlas guidance**—so narrow benchmark results are never mistaken for production adoption or binding rules, and Atlas suggestions remain clearly attributed.

An industry claim is not a regulatory obligation. A community observation is not universal proof. Research paper leads and Atlas guidance require domain validation before production use, and users should verify original primary sources before taking material action.

## How we reference others
We link to original publications and write our own concise summaries, crediting the author or company. We do not reproduce full posts, diagrams, proprietary rules or documents without appropriate rights. Attribution alone does not provide copyright permission.
