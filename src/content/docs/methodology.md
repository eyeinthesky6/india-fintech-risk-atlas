title: Methodology
---

The Atlas is an India-focused **reference platform first** (with candidate solutions second), not a compliance or advisory service. Each article separates **five evidence layers**: **Regulatory** (binding mandates and official guidance scoped by entity class and date), **Industry practice** (`company-reported` or `vendor-reported` engineering practices), **Community insight** (attributed firsthand public observations), **Research & academic** (narrow empirical or `arXiv` benchmark leads), and **Atlas guidance** (editorial synthesis, candidate gap controls, telemetry prerequisites, and validation caveats).

Records follow a **Ready-Reckoner** layout for quick lookup: plain-English mechanism and impact upfront, a colour-keyed **What to do** (`Basis | Owner | Action`) checklist, operational watch-outs, and a collapsed **Sources, legal scope & review history** drawer at the bottom alongside the Record Dossier and best-effort disclaimer. A citation must support the exact claim and entity class; a rule governing a bank does not automatically govern a merchant or TPAP.

**Publication gate:** Primary source check, entity-scope and effective-date check, independent editorial review, and a passing site build. A successful build is not proof of factual accuracy; every page links directly to a **Report a factual error** issue form and GitHub source editing for human corrections.
