---
title: Methodology
---

The Atlas is an India-only public research library, not a compliance tool. Each article distinguishes **binding requirements**, **official guidance**, **industry practices**, and **illustrative hypotheses**.

Sources are cited inline and listed at the bottom of each page. A citation must support the exact claim and the applicable entity class; a rule governing a bank does not automatically govern a merchant or third-party application.

**Publication gate:** Source review, scope/effective-date check, independent editorial review, and a passing site build. A successful build is not proof of factual accuracy.
