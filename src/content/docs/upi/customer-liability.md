## Summary
Fraud reporting and customer liability are distinct from ordinary failed-payment reversal.

## Evidence from primary material
RBI's 2026 customer-protection directions §§76A–76B address bank policies, reporting channels, fraud prevention and grievance mechanisms. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
A reference page should explain where to report, what evidence to retain and which bank policy applies, with current liability provisions checked before quoting numerical periods.

## Limitations and false positives
Authorized payments induced by deception and unauthorized transactions may have different legal treatment; do not conflate them.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
