UPI is an instant-payment system operated by NPCI and used through participating banks and payment applications. Responsibilities vary among NPCI, banks, PSP banks, third-party app providers, merchants and other entities. [NPCI product overview](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview).
