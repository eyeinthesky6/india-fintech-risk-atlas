## Summary
Authentication establishes that the payment instruction is authorized through applicable factors and mechanisms.

## Evidence from primary material
RBI's 2025 directions specify broad authentication principles for covered payment providers and participants, with compliance by 1 April 2026 unless otherwise indicated. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Document actual participant obligations, available authentication options and risk-based safeguards separately.

## Limitations and false positives
Do not infer that any single OTP or PIN flow is universally prescribed across all exempted and covered cases.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
