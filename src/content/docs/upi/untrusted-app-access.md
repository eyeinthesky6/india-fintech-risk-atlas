**ID:** UPI-008 · **Category:** Account security · **Status:** Research draft — independent review pending

## Summary
A user may be persuaded to install an unknown application that increases exposure to credential theft or unauthorized device access.

## Evidence from primary material
NPCI cautions users about downloading unknown apps. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Device integrity and suspicious app behavior can inform risk assessment where lawful and technically supported.

## Limitations and false positives
Accessibility tools and legitimate support apps can resemble risk signals; avoid indiscriminate blocking.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
