## Four evidence layers

| Layer | What it means | Typical attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Official** | Regulator or scheme operator's requirement or guidance, scoped to applicable entities and dates | RBI/NPCI, document, section and date |
| **Industry** | Published company implementation or case study | Company and original article; company-reported |
| **Practitioner** | A named individual's public observation or proposed technique | Author, publication date and original link |
| **Atlas analysis** | Our synthesis or hypothesis, including future AI-assisted ideas | Atlas interpretation, evidence links and reviewer |

An industry claim is not a regulatory obligation. A practitioner's observation is not proof of universal effectiveness. An AI-assisted hypothesis is not a verified fact.

## How we reference others
We link to original publications and write our own concise summaries, crediting the author or company. We do not reproduce full posts, diagrams, proprietary rules or documents without appropriate rights. Attribution alone does not provide copyright permission.
