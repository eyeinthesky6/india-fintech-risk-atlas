title: Methodology
---

The Atlas is an India-focused **reference platform first** (with candidate solutions second), not a compliance tool. Each article strictly separates four evidence layers: **Official** (regulatory/scheme obligations and guidance), **Industry** (`company-reported` engineering practices), **Practitioner** (attributed firsthand operational observations), and **Atlas analysis & research references** (candidate controls, operational trade-offs, and academic paper leads for exploring gaps).

Sources are cited inline and listed at the bottom of each page. A citation must support the exact claim and the applicable entity class; a rule governing a bank does not automatically govern a merchant or third-party application.

**Publication gate:** Source review, scope/effective-date check, independent editorial review, and a passing site build. A successful build is not proof of factual accuracy.
