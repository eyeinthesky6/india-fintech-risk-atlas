**ID:** UPI-004 · **Type:** Fraud pattern · **Basis:** Illustrative hypothesis · **Status:** Research preview

## Pattern
A person may be misled about whether a payment interaction will debit or credit their account. Clear payment-intent communication is an important consumer-protection design consideration.

## Candidate checks
Make the transaction direction and payee understandable; consider contextual warnings and accessible confirmation steps. Specific obligations vary by participant and must be sourced before being described as mandatory.

## False positives
Legitimate payment requests between known parties.
