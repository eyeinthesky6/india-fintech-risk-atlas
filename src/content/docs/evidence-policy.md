India Fintech Risk Atlas is a **reference platform first**; candidate solutions come second. Every record separates verified reference facts from company practices, practitioner accounts, and research ideas across **four evidence layers**:

## Four evidence layers

| Layer | What it means | Typical attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Official** | Regulator or scheme operator's requirement or guidance, scoped to applicable entities and dates | RBI/NPCI, document, section and date |
| **Industry** | Published company implementation or case study | Company and original article; `company-reported` |
| **Practitioner** | A named individual's firsthand public observation or operational lesson | Author, publication date and original link |
| **Atlas analysis & research refs** | Our synthesis, candidate gap solutions, and academic/research paper leads (`arXiv`, etc.) | Atlas interpretation, paper citation, caveats, and reviewer |

Academic and empirical papers sit under **Layer 4** as candidate ideas for exploring operational gaps—not under **Industry** or **Practitioner**, because papers are typically narrow, short-objective studies rather than ready-to-use production controls.

An industry claim is not a regulatory obligation. A practitioner observation is not universal proof. Research paper leads and Atlas hypotheses require domain validation before production use.

## How we reference others
We link to original publications and write our own concise summaries, crediting the author or company. We do not reproduce full posts, diagrams, proprietary rules or documents without appropriate rights. Attribution alone does not provide copyright permission.
