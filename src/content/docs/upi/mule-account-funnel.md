**ID:** UPI-002 · **Type:** Fraud pattern · **Basis:** Illustrative hypothesis · **Status:** Research preview

## Pattern
An account receives payments from several unrelated counterparties and quickly sends funds onward. This may be consistent with pass-through behavior, but is not proof of wrongdoing.

## Candidate checks
Examine account tenure, fund-flow concentration, beneficiary diversity and lawful contextual signals. Human review should consider the purpose of the account and the nature of the business.

## False positives
Collection merchants, community fundraisers and shared expense organizers may show similar flows.
