**ID:** UPI-011 · **Category:** Detective control · **Status:** Research draft — independent review pending

## Summary
Repeated failed attempts may indicate user error, account-access problems or malicious activity.

## Evidence from primary material
RBI payments-bank directions §52 discuss mechanisms to alert customers about failed authentication attempts; the scope is payments banks. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Consider user notifications, contextual investigation and proportionate safeguards where relevant.

## Limitations and false positives
Mistyped credentials, connectivity issues and accessibility challenges can cause benign failures.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
