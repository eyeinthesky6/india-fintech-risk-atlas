**ID:** UPI-007 · **Category:** Social engineering · **Status:** Research draft — independent review pending

## Summary
Scammers may approach users through search results or social media conversations pretending to resolve a failed transaction or refund.

## Evidence from primary material
NPCI's public fraud-awareness material identifies search-result and grievance-related deception as risks. [1]

## Potential checks and operational considerations
Use verified support channels, avoid sharing credentials and design clear in-app complaint journeys.

## Limitations and false positives
Not every third-party support interaction is fraudulent; verify identity and channel.

## Applicability
This article does not assert a universal mandatory control. Applicability depends on the participant's regulated role and the exact source provision.
