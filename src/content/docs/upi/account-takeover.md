**ID:** UPI-003 · **Type:** Fraud pattern · **Basis:** Illustrative hypothesis · **Status:** Research preview

## Pattern
An unauthorized actor gains control of an account or payment credentials and attempts transactions. Changes in authentication context may be relevant but do not establish compromise by themselves.

## Candidate checks
Consider device and credential changes alongside transaction anomalies, customer reports and verified authentication events. Apply data minimization and proportionate response.

## False positives
Legitimate phone upgrades, travel, account recovery and accessibility changes.
